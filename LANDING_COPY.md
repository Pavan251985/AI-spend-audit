## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**
A: Yes. No credit card, no signup required. You only optionally
share your email after seeing your results.

**Q: How accurate is the pricing data?**
A: Every price is pulled from the official vendor pricing page and
verified weekly. Sources are cited in our pricing data file.

**Q: Will you sell my data?**
A: No. Your audit data is used only to generate your results and
optionally send you a copy by email.

**Q: What is Credex?**
A: Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits — the same
tools you use, at up to 40% off retail price. This tool is free
because it helps us find people who are overspending and show them
a better option.

**Q: What if my spend is already optimized?**
A: We will tell you honestly. We do not manufacture fake savings.
If you are spending well, we will say so and offer to notify you
when new optimizations apply to your stack.

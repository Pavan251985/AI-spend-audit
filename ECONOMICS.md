 # Economics

## What a Converted Lead is Worth to Credex

Credex sells discounted AI credits. Assume:
- Average deal size: $2,000/year in credits purchased
- Gross margin on credits: 20% (buying at discount, selling below retail)
- Revenue per customer: $400/year gross profit
- Customer lifetime: 2 years average
- LTV per customer: $800

This is conservative. Enterprise customers buying $10,000+/year in credits
would have LTV of $4,000+.

## CAC at Each Channel

| Channel | Cost | Conversions | CAC |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hacker News post | $0 | 5 consultations | $0 |
| Twitter thread | $0 | 3 consultations | $0 |
| Reddit posts | $0 | 2 consultations | $0 |
| LinkedIn outreach | $0 (time only) | 2 consultations | $0 |
| Credex email list | $0 | 10 consultations | $0 |

At $0 paid CAC, every conversion is pure profit. The tool itself costs
~$20/month to run (Vercel free tier, Supabase free tier, Resend free tier).

## Conversion Funnel

| Stage | Number | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Visitors | 1,000 | 100% |
| Audits completed | 300 | 30% |
| Emails captured | 90 | 30% of audits |
| Consultations booked | 9 | 10% of emails |
| Credits purchased | 3 | 33% of consultations |
| Revenue | $6,000/year | — |

At scale (10,000 visitors/month):
- 3,000 audits
- 900 emails
- 90 consultations
- 30 purchases
- $60,000 ARR from one channel

## What Would Have to Be True for $1M ARR in 18 Months

**Math:**
- $1M ARR / $2,000 average deal = 500 customers
- 500 customers / 18 months = 28 new customers/month
- At 33% close rate: 84 consultations/month
- At 10% consultation rate: 840 emails/month
- At 30% email capture: 2,800 audits/month
- At 30% audit completion: 9,300 visitors/month

**What has to be true:**
1. The tool gets to 10,000 visitors/month within 6 months — achievable with
   consistent content and community posting
2. Credex closes 1 in 3 consultations — requires a strong sales process
3. Average deal size stays at $2,000+ — requires targeting teams of 5+
4. The audit engine recommendations are accurate enough that users trust
   Credex's advice — requires keeping pricing data current

$1M ARR in 18 months is aggressive but plausible if Credex already has
brand recognition in the AI infrastructure space and a warm customer list
to seed initial traction.

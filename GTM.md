# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Exact Target User

Not "startups" — the exact user is an **engineering manager or CTO at a Series A startup with 5-20 engineers**, paying for 3+ AI tools across their team. They approved the AI tool budget 6 months ago when the team was smaller and haven't revisited it since. They see the monthly bill, assume it's justified, and move on. They are not a finance person — they are technical — but they feel vague guilt about not optimizing costs.

Job title: Engineering Manager, VP Engineering, or CTO
Company stage: Series A, 10-50 employees
Location: US, UK, India
Pain: No single view of AI tool spend across the team

## How to Get First 100 Users in 30 Days with $0

**Week 1 — Hacker News:**
Post "Show HN: I built a free AI spend auditor for engineering teams" with a genuine story about discovering overspend. HN loves tools that solve real problems transparently. Target: 50 upvotes, 500 visits.

**Week 2 — Twitter/X:**
Tweet a thread: "I audited 10 startups AI tool spend. Here's what I found:" with real anonymized data from early users. Tag relevant accounts. Target: 20 retweets, 200 clicks.

**Week 3 — Reddit:**
Post in r/ExperiencedDevs and r/startups with genuine value: "Built a free tool to audit your AI tool spend — no signup required." Be transparent about Credex connection. Target: 100 upvotes, 300 visits.

**Week 4 — Direct outreach:**
DM 50 engineering managers on LinkedIn with a personalized message referencing their company's known tech stack. "I noticed you use Cursor — curious if you've audited your AI spend lately." Target: 10 responses, 5 consultations booked.

## The Unfair Distribution Channel
